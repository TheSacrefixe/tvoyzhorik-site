You do not need an account or registration to ask us to delete data. Choose the section that fits you.

Connecting platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok) and replying to customers in Instagram Direct and Messenger are not yet available — we will tell you in the chat when they are; the periods for platform and customer data will apply from the first day of such a connection. During the pilot, some deletions (including "Forget everything" and clearing logs after 30 days) are done manually by our team — within the same periods.

A. You use Tvii Zhoryk (business owner or team member)

Option 1 — in the chat. Write to the assistant in Telegram:

  • "Forget …" and the fact you want removed — it is deleted from the assistant's notes;
  • "Forget everything" — deletes your business's notes, settings, conversation and connector logs, data from connected platforms and data about your buyers, and revokes platform access. Only billing records are kept, for as long as Ukrainian tax law requires. The owner confirms with a button; deletion is completed within 72 hours.

Option 2 — disconnect a platform. Write "Disconnect Google", "Disconnect Meta" or "Disconnect TikTok", or remove access in the platform's own settings:

  • Google: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations → select the app → Remove (https://www.facebook.com/help/405094243235242)
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Active → Remove (https://help.instagram.com/1144624522593085)
  • TikTok: write "Disconnect TikTok" in the chat.

We revoke our access token the same day and, within 3 business days, delete the data received from that platform, including notes the assistant made from it. The conversation log and the connector logs (what the assistant did and with which platform), which may contain replies with figures from that platform, are deleted automatically within 30 days.

Option 3 — by e-mail. Write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com from any address and include your Telegram username or the name of your business. An owner's request to delete everything sent by email is handled the same way — within 72 hours after we confirm it comes from the owner.

B. You are a buyer or follower of a business that uses Tvii Zhoryk

If you commented on, messaged or ordered from a business that uses our assistant on Instagram, Facebook or elsewhere, the business may have shared some of that information with the assistant. If you wrote to the business in Instagram Direct or Messenger, where our assistant answers on the business's behalf, the assistant received your conversation directly from Instagram or Messenger (Meta), not from the business; we keep it for 30 days and then delete it automatically. You can ask us to delete this data — no registration needed.

Send an e-mail to hello@tvoyzhorik.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us:

  1. the name of the business (or its Instagram / Facebook page);
  2. your username or name on that platform;
  3. if you can — roughly when you contacted the business.

If you wrote to the business in Instagram Direct or Messenger, you can simply write “delete my data” to the assistant in the same conversation, even if you wrote “stop” before. It will reply right away in the same conversation that your request has been accepted, with a link to this page, and will pass your request to us itself. We delete your data within 30 days (in practice sooner); what exactly we delete is listed below. We confirm that your data has been deleted in the same conversation if it is still open for replies (Meta's standard messaging window — for most user actions it lasts 24 hours). If the window has already closed, the assistant does not write to you in that conversation on its own (outside the window, Meta allows only a reply from a person on the business's team, within 7 days); to get a confirmation, write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com (if you also wrote to us by e-mail, we confirm by e-mail). If you did not write in Direct or Messenger, use the e-mail above.

We will not ask for passwords, codes or documents. If we cannot find your data with these details, we will tell you what else would help.

C. You left your contact on the waitlist (the “Closed pilot” page)

Write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com with the subject “Delete my entry” and include the contact you left in the form (Telegram username or e-mail). If we have already written to you, just reply “delete” in the same conversation.

We delete within 3 business days and confirm. If you joined through the bot @TvoyJorik_bot, send it /delete. Without a request, the entry is deleted automatically 12 months after you joined. What exactly we store — Privacy Policy, subsection 3.6.

What we delete

At your request we delete what we hold about you:

  • your conversation with the assistant in Instagram Direct or Messenger (even without a request it is kept only 30 days); we do not build a profile or customer card about you from platform data;
  • technical identifiers: the ID Instagram or Messenger assigns to you for this business, your name and username there;
  • entries about you in the conversation and connector logs;
  • files linked to you: photos and other attachments you sent, screenshots and order details about you that the business shared with the assistant;
  • messages and comments about you that the business shared with the assistant, and notes the assistant made about you;
  • data about you that we received from Instagram, Facebook, Google or TikTok.

All of this is deleted within 30 days of your request, including your ID. If you write to the business again afterwards, the assistant treats you as a new person and introduces itself again. If there was no request, the conversation and connector logs are deleted automatically within 30 days. Notes the assistant made about a buyer from the business's own data are kept while the business is our customer and for 30 days after it leaves; at your request they are deleted earlier, within 30 days.

What we keep:

  • if you wrote “stop” and did not ask us to delete your data, we keep your ID only while the stop is in effect, so that the assistant does not reply to you; we keep your ID, not your name, messages or history. “Start” or a request to delete your data removes it; it is also deleted when the business disconnects the platform from our service;
  • a record that your data was deleted: only the date, the platform and the categories of deleted data — without your ID, without your name or the content; we keep it for 12 months as proof that your request was handled, then delete it;
  • if the law requires us to keep a record (for example, an invoice), only that record, and we tell you so.

Separately:

  • Backups of our server: data disappears from them automatically when the backups expire — no later than 5 weeks.
  • The conversation itself also stays in Instagram or Messenger, in the inbox of the business and in yours. We delete our copies, but we cannot delete it there — you can delete it in your own app, and the business in its own.
  • What the business saved or forwarded to itself (for example, a screenshot of your conversation) is deleted by the business. We delete our copy and forward your request to the business so it can act on it too.
  • The assistant's answers are written by the AI model Claude by Anthropic through its commercial API. Under Anthropic's published rules, it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, with exceptions, for example to enforce its Usage Policy (if a request is flagged as a violation — up to 2 years): https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data

How long it takes

  • We confirm we received your request within 3 business days.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days, and we send you a confirmation by e-mail.
  • If you asked in Instagram Direct or Messenger, the assistant replies right away in the same conversation that your request has been accepted; deletion is completed within 30 days; we confirm it in the same conversation if Meta's messaging window (usually 24 hours) is still open, otherwise — at your request to hello@tvoyzhorik.com.
