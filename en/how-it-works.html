One day of a business owner

How it works

The same day twice: the usual way — and as one chat with the assistant in Telegram.

Example: a gym. The examples are made up — to show what it looks like. What exactly your assistant does depends on its skills: ready-made ones are in the Library, and the team will add the one your business needs on request.

Step 0

Your assistant is set up personally for your business

It is not a one-size-fits-all template: the assistant knows your business —

  • Your products and services

    What you sell, what is in stock, what is on the timetable.

  • Your prices

    And the rules: where a discount is possible, and where only after your “Yes”.

  • Your team

    Who is responsible for what and who gets which task.

  • Your tone

    How you talk to clients: casual or formal, with a joke or strictly by the book.

All of it comes from what you tell it and what you connect. Something changed? Tell it, and it will take that into account.

Getting started

Three steps, and you’re connected

Three stops — and the assistant is ready to work.

  1. You start a chat with the assistant in Telegram and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

  2. You connect the services you want — Google (Ads, Analytics, Search Console, Merchant Center, Business Profile), Meta (Instagram, Facebook Pages, Instagram Direct and Messenger messages, advertising), TikTok ads — each through its own official sign-in. You can disconnect any of them at any time.

  3. You decide what the assistant may do on its own; anything involving money needs your confirmation, except Meta and TikTok ad budgets within the monthly envelope you approved (see the Terms of Service, section 4).

Who decides what

  1. Step 1The assistant prepares

Money and publishing happen only after your “Yes” — given each time, or once for a budget or content plan you have approved. Details are in the Terms of Service.
