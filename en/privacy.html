This policy explains what data Tvii Zhoryk processes, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. If anything is unclear, write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com.

1. Who we are

Tvii Zhoryk is an AI assistant for small business owners and their teams. It works in Telegram and, when the business owner connects them, with the owner's advertising, analytics and social media accounts.

The service is operated by FOP Oliinyk Kateryna, tax ID (RNOKPP) 3341011642, address: Україна, 54036, Миколаївська обл., м. Миколаїв, пров. Contact for privacy questions and deletion requests: hello@tvoyzhorik.com.

2. Our role

  • For the account data of our customers (the business owner and the team members they invite) we decide how the data is used — we are the controller.
  • For data that belongs to our customer's business — its advertising accounts, social media pages, analytics, CRM, and messages or comments from the business's own buyers — we act on the customer's instructions and on their behalf, as a processor / service provider. The business is responsible for having the right to share that data with us.

3. What data we collect

3.1 From the business owner and team members (Telegram)

  • Telegram user ID, name and username, language.
  • Messages, photos, files and documents you send to the assistant, and the assistant's replies. Voice messages — once support for them is launched (see section 7).
  • Settings: roles and permissions of team members, preferences, confirmations you give with buttons, the version of the Terms and this Policy you accepted.
  • Billing: plan, payment dates, payment status and the transaction reference. We do not receive or store card numbers.

Please do not send the assistant card numbers, one-time codes, passwords or access keys. The assistant will never ask for them; if it notices one in a message, it will warn you.

3.2 From connected platforms

We access a platform only after the business owner connects it with the platform's own sign-in and consent screen. Each platform is described separately in section 4.

3.3 About the business's buyers

The business may share with the assistant information about its own buyers — for example, a screenshot of a buyer's message or comment, an order from its CRM, or comments under its posts received from a connected platform. Such data may include a name, username, message text and order details. We process it only to do the task the business asked for. If the owner connects Instagram Direct or Messenger, the assistant also receives the messages buyers send to the business there and replies to them on the business's behalf (see section 4, Meta). Section 9 explains how a buyer can ask us to delete it without registering.

3.4 If you message our bot @TvoyJorik_bot

We receive your Telegram ID, name, username and your answers to the bot's questions (language, country, what you do, what you want to improve) and, if you give it, your e-mail. We use them only to contact you about the pilot. To delete them, send /delete to the bot — we delete them right away; copies in our team's internal messages are deleted within 3 business days.

4. Data from each platform: what, why, how to disconnect

Google (Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Merchant Center, Google Business Profile)

  • What we access: reports and settings of the accounts the owner selects — campaign and spending statistics (Google Ads), website traffic and conversion reports (GA4), search performance (Search Console), product feed status (Merchant Center), business listing and reviews (Business Profile). We request only the narrowest access needed.
  • Why: to prepare reports, answer the owner's questions about results and suggest improvements. In Google Ads, during the pilot (see the Terms of Service, section 4), the assistant only reads data; it does not change campaigns or budgets.
  • How to disconnect: tell the assistant "Disconnect Google", or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. We then revoke our access token the same day and, within 3 business days, delete the Google data we hold, including notes the assistant made from it. The only exceptions are the conversation log and the connector logs (what the assistant did and with which platform): they may contain the assistant's replies with figures from Google and records of its actions there, and are deleted automatically within 30 days (see section 8).

Meta (Instagram professional accounts, Facebook Pages, Meta advertising)

  • What we access: the owner's Instagram professional account and Facebook Page — posts, insights and comments; advertising accounts — campaigns, results and spending.
  • Why: to prepare reports, draft content, publish posts only from a content plan the owner approved, and change advertising budgets only within the monthly budget ("envelope") the owner approved with a button. Only the owner can approve or change the envelope.
  • Messages from buyers (Instagram Direct, Messenger): if the owner connects them, we receive the messages buyers send to the business — the buyer's name and username on the platform, the ID the platform assigns to the buyer for this business, the message text and attachments (for example, photos) — to answer buyers on the business's behalf. The assistant introduces itself as “AI assistant of the business's brand name” in its first message of a new conversation, after a 24-hour pause and in its first message after a person from the business's team hands the conversation back to it, and when asked whether it is a bot, always answers honestly: “I am an AI assistant”; it hands the conversation to a person from the business's team when the buyer asks (the team replies when available); and when the buyer writes “stop” (or “стоп”), it stops answering in this conversation right away and, until the buyer writes “start” (or “старт”), replies only to service requests — to delete their data, to start again or to talk to a person — briefly and without selling anything, and after “start” it introduces itself again (“I am an AI assistant”), while the buyer's messages still reach the business's team. We keep these conversations for 30 days and then delete them automatically (see section 8); we do not build buyer profiles or customer cards from platform data. A buyer can ask us to delete their data at any time, without registering: https://tvoyzhorik.com/en/data-deletion, section B.
  • How to disconnect: tell the assistant "Disconnect Meta", or remove the app in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (https://www.facebook.com/help/405094243235242), or in Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (https://help.instagram.com/1144624522593085). We then delete the Meta data we hold (see section 8).

TikTok (TikTok advertising)

  • What we access: the owner's TikTok ads account — campaigns, results and spending — through TikTok's own sign-in for business accounts.
  • Why: to prepare reports, suggest changes and change advertising budgets only within the monthly budget ("envelope") the owner approved with a button. Only the owner can approve or change the envelope.
  • How to disconnect: tell the assistant "Disconnect TikTok". We then delete the TikTok data we hold (see section 8).

Other services the owner connects (for example, a CRM): the assistant reads and, after confirmation, updates only what the owner allowed. They are disconnected the same way.

We do not sell any data. We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling or for any purpose other than the service for this particular business. Data of one business is never shown to or used for another business.

5. Google user data — Limited Use

Tvii Zhoryk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular: we use Google user data only to provide the features the owner sees and requested; we do not transfer it except to the service providers in section 6 that are needed to provide those features; we do not use it for advertising; and people on our team do not read it, except with the owner's permission for a specific case, for security reasons, or when required by law.

7. AI and model training

The assistant's answers are generated by the AI model Claude by Anthropic, used through Anthropic's commercial API. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services" (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms).

Your data and data from connected platforms are not used to train AI models — neither by us nor by Anthropic. Source: Anthropic Commercial Terms, section B "Customer Content": https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms

Voice messages are not yet supported in the pilot.

9. How to delete your data

  • In the chat: tell the assistant "Forget …" (a specific fact, or everything). Deleting everything is confirmed by the owner with a button and is completed within 72 hours: we delete notes, settings, the conversation and connector logs, data from connected platforms and data about the business's buyers, and revoke platform access. Only billing records are kept, for as long as Ukrainian tax law requires (see section 8).
  • Disconnect a platform: see section 4.
  • By e-mail: hello@tvoyzhorik.com.
  • If you are a buyer of a business that uses Tvii Zhoryk: see https://tvoyzhorik.com/en/data-deletion — no registration needed.

Full instructions: https://tvoyzhorik.com/en/data-deletion

10. Security

  • Each business's data is kept separately from other businesses' data on our server.
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Access tokens of connected platforms are stored encrypted.
  • Our team does not see the content of your conversations by default. If a team member needs to look at a specific case (for example, to fix an error), the data is shown in de-identified form, only for a stated reason, and every such access is recorded in a log.
  • To report a security vulnerability: hello@tvoyzhorik.com.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete your data, and you can withdraw a platform's access at any time. Write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com; we answer within 3 business days and complete the request within 30 days. An owner's request to delete everything sent by email is handled the same way — within 72 hours after we confirm it comes from the owner.

12. Children

The service is for businesses. It is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes to this Policy

The version and date are at the top of this page. If we change how we use your data — in particular Google user data — we will tell you in the chat and ask you to accept the new version before we use data in a new way. Previous versions are available on request.
