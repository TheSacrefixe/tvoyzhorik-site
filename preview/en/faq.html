Frequently asked questions

Tap a question and the assistant answers in its own words. Everything here comes from our Terms and Policy; if anything differs, they prevail.

  • What can you do?
    Zhoryk

    I answer questions about your business, prepare reports and draft content. When you connect them, I work with your advertising, analytics, social media and CRM accounts within the permissions you give, and I answer your buyers in Instagram Direct and Messenger.

    I don’t provide legal, tax or financial advice. And I’m AI — I can make mistakes, so check important figures.

  • Who presses “Yes”?
    Zhoryk

    You do. I read data from connected accounts on my own. A new type of action — for example, the first publication or the first change to stock levels — only after your button. I publish only from a content plan you approved.

    Anything that moves money — only with a button, every time; the exception is Meta and TikTok advertising within the monthly budget you approved. Deleting — always with a double confirmation. I prepare — you decide.

  • What happens to my data?
    Zhoryk

    It works only for your business. Each business’s data is kept separately; we don’t sell it and don’t use it to train AI models — neither we nor Anthropic.

    Card numbers, one-time codes and passwords in messages are masked automatically. Our server is in Germany (EU). Details — in the Privacy Policy.

  • How do I delete my data?
    Zhoryk

    Write “Forget everything” to me — I’ll delete your notes, settings, conversation and connection logs, data from connected platforms and data about your buyers, and revoke platform access. You confirm with a button; deletion is completed within 72 hours. Only billing records are kept, for as long as Ukrainian tax law requires.

    Or write to hello@tvoyzhorik.com. All the options are on the How to delete your data page.

  • Which languages do you speak?
    Zhoryk

    This site and all our documents are in Ukrainian and English. The Terms don’t list languages, so ask us about yours in Telegram — we’ll answer honestly.

  • How much does it cost?
    Zhoryk

    Prices are in Telegram — write to us. Prices and plans are shown in the chat before payment; billing is monthly.

  • What if you make a mistake?
    Zhoryk

    I can — I’m powered by an AI model. So check important facts, figures and texts before relying on them. And I don’t do anything that moves money without your button.

The answers briefly retell the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and How to delete your data.
