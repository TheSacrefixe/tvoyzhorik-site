One day of a business owner

How it works

The same day twice: the usual way — and as one chat with the assistant in Telegram.

Example: a gym; names, prices and numbers are made up.

Step 0

Your assistant is set up personally for your business

It is not a one-size-fits-all template: the assistant knows your business —

  • Your products and services

    What you sell, what is in stock, what is on the timetable.

  • Your prices

    And the rules: where a discount is possible, and where only after your “Yes”.

  • Your team

    Who is responsible for what and who gets which task.

  • Your tone

    How you talk to clients: casual or formal, with a joke or strictly by the book.

All of it comes from what you tell it and what you connect. Something changed? Tell it, and it will take that into account.

One voice message — three tasks
