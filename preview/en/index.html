Tvii Zhoryk

An AI assistant in Telegram that takes routine work off a small business owner.

What it does

Tvii Zhoryk works in a Telegram chat with the business owner and their team. You write or send a voice message in your own words — the assistant does the task or prepares it for your approval:

  • Reports in plain words. Advertising results, website traffic and search performance — from the accounts you connect.
  • Content. Drafts of posts and stories; publishes to your Instagram or Facebook Page only from a content plan you approved.
  • Replies to your buyers in Instagram Direct and Messenger. If you connect them, the assistant answers your buyers' messages on behalf of your business and introduces itself as “AI assistant of your brand name” in its first message of a new conversation, after a 24-hour pause and in its first message after a person from your team hands the conversation back to it, and when asked whether it is a bot, always answers honestly: “I am an AI assistant”. A buyer can ask for a person at any time — the assistant hands the conversation to your team, who will reply when they are available. If a buyer writes “stop” (or “стоп”), the assistant stops answering in this conversation right away and, until the buyer writes “start” (or “старт”), replies only to service requests — to delete their data, to start again or to talk to a person — briefly and without selling anything, and after “start” it introduces itself again (“I am an AI assistant”); the buyer's messages still reach your team. A buyer can ask us to delete their data: How to delete your data, section B.
  • Advertising under your control. In Meta advertising, the assistant changes budgets on its own only within the monthly budget you approved with a button. In Google Ads, during the pilot (see the Terms of Service, section 4), it only reads results. In TikTok ads the same rule applies: the assistant changes budgets on its own only within the monthly budget you approved with a button.
  • CRM and routine. Checks stock and orders in your CRM, reminders, answers to your team's questions.
  • Voice. Understands voice messages and can answer by voice; voice is processed on our own server.

Who it is for

Owners of small businesses — shops, brands, service companies — and the people on their team.

How it works

  1. You start a chat with the assistant in Telegram and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  2. You connect the services you want — Google (Ads, Analytics, Search Console, Merchant Center, Business Profile), Meta (Instagram, Facebook Pages, Instagram Direct and Messenger messages, advertising), TikTok ads — each through its own official sign-in. You can disconnect any of them at any time.
  3. You decide what the assistant may do on its own; anything involving money needs your confirmation, except Meta and TikTok ad budgets within the monthly envelope you approved (see the Terms of Service, section 4).

Your data

  • Data from your accounts is used only to work for your business and is never used to train AI models.
  • Each business's data is kept separately.
  • You can delete your data at any time: How to delete your data.

The assistant's answers are generated by the AI model Claude by Anthropic.
