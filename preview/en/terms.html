2. The service

Tvii Zhoryk is an AI assistant in Telegram. It answers questions about the business, prepares reports, drafts content and, when the owner connects them, works with the owner's advertising, analytics, social media and CRM accounts within the permissions the owner gives. If the owner connects Instagram Direct or Messenger, the assistant also answers the Customer's buyers there on the Customer's behalf (section 5.7).

The assistant is powered by an AI model. AI can make mistakes: check important facts, figures and texts before relying on them. The service does not provide legal, tax or financial advice.

3. What the assistant does on its own and what needs your confirmation

  • Reading data from connected accounts — on its own.
  • A new type of action (for example, the first publication or the first update in a CRM) — only after the owner confirms it with a button.
  • Publishing content — only from a content plan the owner approved.
  • Anything that moves money — only with a button, every time. The only exceptions are Meta and TikTok advertising within the envelope the owner approved, described in section 4.
  • Deleting — always with a double confirmation.
  • Once a month the assistant shows the owner a list of what it does on its own; the owner can withdraw any of it with one message.

4. Advertising

  • Meta advertising: the assistant changes budgets only within the monthly advertising budget (the "envelope") that the owner approved with a button. Only the owner can approve or change the envelope. The assistant keeps the account spending limit and the daily pace within the envelope.
  • Google Ads: during the pilot (the "pilot" is the period from [ДАТА ПУБЛИКАЦИИ] to 30 December 2026), the assistant only reads data and does not make changes. If this changes, including after the pilot ends (for example, if the assistant starts managing Google Ads), it will happen only through a new version of these Terms: we will publish it and ask the owner to accept it with a button (section 12).
  • TikTok advertising: as with Meta, the assistant changes budgets only within the monthly advertising budget (the "envelope") that the owner approved with a button. Only the owner can approve or change the envelope.
  • The Customer is the advertiser and remains responsible for its advertising, its content, targeting, and compliance with advertising law and platform advertising policies.
