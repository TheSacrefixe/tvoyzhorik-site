Privacy Policy — Tvii Zhoryk

Version 1.0 · Effective date: [ДАТА ПУБЛИКАЦИИ] · Last updated: [ДАТА ПУБЛИКАЦИИ]

This policy explains what data Tvii Zhoryk processes, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. If anything is unclear, write to privacy@tvoyzhorik.com.

1. Who we are

Tvii Zhoryk is an AI assistant for small business owners and their teams. It works in Telegram and, when the business owner connects them, with the owner's advertising, analytics and social media accounts.

The service is operated by [ОПЕРАТОР: ФИО ФОП, ІПН, адрес] ("we", "us"), a sole proprietor registered in Ukraine. The same name appears on every page of this site, in our Terms of Service and on our invoices.

Contact for privacy questions and deletion requests: privacy@tvoyzhorik.com.

2. Our role

  • For the account data of our customers (the business owner and the team members they invite) we decide how the data is used — we are the controller.
  • For data that belongs to our customer's business — its advertising accounts, social media pages, analytics, CRM, and messages or comments from the business's own buyers — we act on the customer's instructions and on their behalf, as a processor / service provider. The business is responsible for having the right to share that data with us.

3. What data we collect

3.1 From the business owner and team members (Telegram)

  • Telegram user ID, name and username, language.
  • Messages, voice messages, photos, files and documents you send to the assistant, and the assistant's replies.
  • Settings: roles and permissions of team members, preferences, confirmations you give with buttons, the version of the Terms and this Policy you accepted.
  • Billing: plan, payment dates, payment status and the transaction reference. We do not receive or store card numbers.

Card numbers, one-time codes, passwords, keys and tokens that appear in a message are automatically masked before the message is stored or sent to the AI model. [ПРОВЕРИТЬ: что должен делать код — маскировать номера карт, коды, пароли, ключи и токены до записи и до отправки в модель (обещание 4 отчёта Недоверчивого 21.11)]

3.2 From connected platforms

We access a platform only after the business owner connects it with the platform's own sign-in and consent screen. Each platform is described separately in section 4.

3.3 About the business's buyers

The business may share with the assistant information about its own buyers — for example, a screenshot of a buyer's message or comment, an order from its CRM, or comments under its posts received from a connected platform. Such data may include a name, username, message text and order details. We process it only to do the task the business asked for. If the owner connects Instagram Direct or Messenger, the assistant also receives the messages buyers send to the business there and replies to them on the business's behalf (see section 4, Meta). Section 9 explains how a buyer can ask us to delete it without registering.

Google (Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Merchant Center, Google Business Profile) - What we access: reports and settings of the accounts the owner selects — campaign and spending statistics (Google Ads), website traffic and conversion reports (GA4), search performance (Search Console), product feed status (Merchant Center), business listing and reviews (Business Profile). We request only the narrowest access needed. [ПРОВЕРИТЬ: точный список scopes по каждому API, когда будет экран согласия] - Why: to prepare reports, answer the owner's questions about results and suggest improvements. The only exceptions are the conversation log and the connector logs (what the assistant did and with which platform): they may contain the assistant's replies with figures from Google and records of its actions there, and are deleted automatically within 30 days (see section 8). In particular: we use Google user data only to provide the features the owner sees and requested; we do not transfer it except to the service providers in section 6 that are needed to provide those features; we do not use it for advertising; and people on our team do not read it, except with the owner's permission for a specific case, for security reasons, or when required by law. 7. AI and model training

The assistant's answers are generated by the AI model Claude by Anthropic, used through Anthropic's commercial API. Under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services" (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms).

Your data and data from connected platforms are not used to train AI models — neither by us nor by Anthropic. Source: Anthropic Commercial Terms, section B "Customer Content": https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms

Voice messages are recognised and voice replies are generated on our own server. Audio is not sent to any outside speech service and is deleted after it is converted to text. 9. How to delete your data

  • In the chat: tell the assistant "Forget …" (a specific fact, or everything). Deleting everything is confirmed by the owner with a button and is completed within 72 hours: we delete notes, settings, the conversation and connector logs, data from connected platforms and data about the business's buyers, and revoke platform access. Only billing records are kept, for as long as Ukrainian tax law requires (see section 8). [ПРОВЕРИТЬ: что должен делать код — «Забудь всё» за ≤72 ч удаляет заметки, настройки, журналы, данные платформ и покупателей, отзывает токены, оставляет только платёжные записи; сейчас это план (N-04/05), не код (обещание 2)]
  • Disconnect a platform: see section 4.
  • By e-mail: privacy@tvoyzhorik.com.
  • If you are a buyer of a business that uses Tvii Zhoryk: see https://tvoyzhorik.com/data-deletion — no registration needed.

  • Each business's data is kept separately from other businesses' data on our server. [ПРОВЕРИТЬ: что должен делать код — хранить данные каждого бизнеса отдельно (изоляция арендаторов) (обещание 7)]

If a team member needs to look at a specific case (for example, to fix an error), the data is shown in de-identified form, only for a stated reason, and every such access is recorded in a log. [ПРОВЕРИТЬ: что должен делать код — по умолчанию не показывать команде переписку; доступ — обезличенный, с причиной и записью в журнал доступа (обещание 5; для данных Google — условие Limited Use)]
